Arcpact Support

Winter Arc challenge tracker · Elden Studios

Write to bazerbashi@elden-studios.com. We read every message and usually reply within a day. In the app, Settings → Send feedback fills in your app version for us.

I forgot to tick yesterday

Until 12:00 noon you can still finish yesterday: tap Finish yesterday on the Today screen. After noon the day is closed.

Steady or Strict?

Both have the same rules. In Steady, a missed day stays on your record and your arc keeps going. In Strict, a missed day sends you back to Day 1 — or you can switch to Steady and keep your days. Your photos are never deleted either way.

Reminders

You get at most two a day: a nudge at your time, and an evening last call only if the day isn't kept. Change or turn them off in Settings → Reminders.

Cancel or manage a subscription

In the app: Settings → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Arcpact. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. If you start a free trial, the app reminds you two days before it ends (if notifications are on).

Restore purchases

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Where are my photos?

Inside Arcpact on your iPhone, outside your photo library, in the Photos tab. Past arcs are under Settings → Past arcs. Deleting the app deletes them, so export your timelapse first if you want to keep it.

Privacy

No account, no tracking, no data collected. Read the privacy policy.
