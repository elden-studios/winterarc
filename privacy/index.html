Privacy Policy

Arcpact (Winter Arc challenge tracker) · Elden Studios · Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version

Arcpact does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you create in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your arcs: the rules you choose, the days you tick and your settings are saved inside the app on your device.
  • Your progress photos: saved inside the app's private storage, outside your photo library. They are never uploaded. You can lock them with Face ID or your passcode.
  • Backups: like other app data, this information is included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple under your own account.
  • Widgets: your current day and rules are shared with the Arcpact widget on the same device so it can show them.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The app asks for camera access only to take your daily progress photo. To import a photo instead, you pick it in the iOS photo picker, and the app receives only the photo you choose. If you save your timelapse to Photos, iOS asks you for permission to add it. Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. We can see standard, anonymous sales reports from Apple; they do not identify you.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. To remove it from backups too, delete those backups in your iPhone or computer settings. You can also end an arc or delete individual photos inside the app at any time.

Children

Arcpact is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Arcpact is a habit and challenge tracker. It does not give medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new diet or exercise program.
